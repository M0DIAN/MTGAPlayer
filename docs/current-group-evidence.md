upperBound2, absent lowerBound and AllowCancel_No. Group was prioritized over

requests with this common finite structure. No new Mulligan support is added;
v6 London/AcceptHand remains historical evidence only.

does not consult hidden object faces or infer their zones from destination
specifications. It emits request-scoped aliases, original list positions, null
